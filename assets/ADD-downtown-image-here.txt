BPTP DOWNTOWN IMAGE
===================
The site is already wired to show a file named exactly:

    downtown.jpg

To add your official BPTP Downtown photo:
1. Save your image into THIS assets/ folder, named:  downtown.jpg
   (use .jpg — if your file is .png/.webp that's fine too, just rename the
    path in js/data.js from downtown.jpg to your filename)
2. Open the site with Live Server and hard-refresh (Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+R).

Until downtown.jpg is present, the card automatically shows the placeholder
artwork (proj-luxury.svg) — nothing breaks.
Recommended: landscape, ~1600px wide, under 500 KB.
